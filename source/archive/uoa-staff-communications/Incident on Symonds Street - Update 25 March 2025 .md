Kia ora koutou,

We can confirm that one of our staff members was injured and is receiving hospital treatment following the incident on Symonds Street yesterday involving a car and pedestrians. Several of our students were also injured and are recovering at home.

A reminder that the Employee Assistance Programme<[https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/intranet/work-personal/staff-support/eap.html>](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/intranet/work-personal/staff-support/eap.html>) (for staff) and Te Papa Manaaki | Campus Care<[https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/students/student-support/personal-support/te-papa-manaaki-campus-care.html>](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/students/student-support/personal-support/te-papa-manaaki-campus-care.html>) (for students) can provide confidential, professional services to anyone needing support.

External support is also available from these organisations:
Mental Health Crisis Line: 0800 373 7550 
Need to Talk [https://1737.org.nz<https://1737.org.nz/>](https://1737.org.nz<https://1737.org.nz/>) | Call or text 1737 or 0800 1737 1737 
Lifeline: 0800 543 354 Free text 4357 (HELP) 
Youthline: 0800 376 633 Free text 234 

Noho oro mai | Stay well
Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland
