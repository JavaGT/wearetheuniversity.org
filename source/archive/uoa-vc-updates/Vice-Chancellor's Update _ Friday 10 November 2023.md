Kia ora ,

I hope this email finds you well.

The University of Auckland has been working with NZ Police to investigate a
potential security threat made against the University on social media last
night.

The Police have now determined that there is no evidence to suggest the threat
is real.

Students can feel safe coming onto campus today, and exams will proceed as
planned. 

Te Papa Manaaki | Campus Care

I acknowledge the disruption and concern this incident may have caused to our
University community. If you need support or guidance, Te Papa Manaaki | Campus
Care is a free, safe and confidential service to support the health, wellbeing
and safety of everyone at University.

You can contact Campus Care via the online form on the website and a Case
Manager will be in contact with you as soon as possible. Submit a support
request
[[https://protect-au.mimecast.com/s/5ChVCq71EMFwPqwXuZRSU_?domain=uoa.custhelp.com].](https://protect-au.mimecast.com/s/5ChVCq71EMFwPqwXuZRSU_?domain=uoa.custhelp.com].)

For more information, please visit Te Papa Manaaki | Campus Care
[[https://protect-au.mimecast.com/s/ZAjKCr81GMHJ0gJ2szsuRO?domain=uoa.custhelp.com].](https://protect-au.mimecast.com/s/ZAjKCr81GMHJ0gJ2szsuRO?domain=uoa.custhelp.com].)

Our Counselling team also is available today. To reach out to them, please
contact 0800 698 427 / 0800 MY UHCS to make an appointment.

Other student support services
