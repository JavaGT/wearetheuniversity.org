Kia ora tātou,

Mānawa Mai Open Day
On Saturday we welcomed more than 11,000 potential students and their whānau
onto our campuses for Mānawa Mai Open Day; it was a wonderful occasion despite
some wet weather. My thanks and congratulations to the 1,200 staff and 200
students whose contributions made the day possible.

Creating our Pacific Strategy
Work has begun on developing the University's inaugural Pacific Strategy. Under
the leadership of the Pro Vice-Chancellor Pacific, Professor Jemaima
Tiatia-Siau, a project team has been formed to craft a transformational 5-year
strategy that brings to life our commitment, as outlined in Taumata Teitei, to
our Pacific students, staff, and their families and communities.

Farewell to John Hosking, Dean of Science
The time has come to bid farewell to Professor John Hosking, who will retire as
Dean of Science on Friday 6 September. Over the past ten years, John has
provided exceptional leadership as Dean of Science, advancing the faculty's
achievements and reputation while inspiring countless students. He has expertly
balanced the demands of leading a comprehensive faculty while excelling in his
research work. I want to acknowledge and thank John for his outstanding
contributions and wish him all the best in his well-earned retirement. Professor
Sarah Young, who joins us in October, will succeed John as Dean of Science.

Ngā mihi nui,

Dawn

Professor Dawn Freshwater
