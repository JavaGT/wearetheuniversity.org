New role for Professor Pene Mathew

I am pleased to announce that Professor Pene Mathew, Dean of Law, has been
appointed as Human Rights Commissioner and President of the ACT Human Rights
Commission in the Australian Capital Territory. In this important and exciting
role, Pene will be working with human rights in a practical, impactful way for
the benefit of residents of the Territory, the first Australian jurisdiction to
adopt a Human Rights Act.

Pene will start in her new position in late October 2023, at which time
Professor Warren Swain will take on the role of Acting Dean of Law. I would like
to acknowledge and thank Pene for her outstanding leadership of the Law Faculty
over her tenure.
