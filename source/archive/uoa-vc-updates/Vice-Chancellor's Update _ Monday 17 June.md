Update on the proposed faculty arrangements

The University Council has approved the creation of a new Faculty, effective
January 2025. It will combine the current Faculties of Education and Social Work
(EDSW) and Arts, and the Fine Arts, Music and Dance disciplines in Creative Arts
and Industries (CAI). The School of Architecture and Planning and the Design
Programme will align with the Faculty of Engineering.

Over the next six months, work will begin to establish the new Faculty,
retaining existing schools and academic units during the transition. A new Dean
will be appointed to lead the new Faculty. I want to thank the current Faculty
Deans for their strategic leadership across their respective departments and the
valuable contributions that colleagues have made throughout this process.

Chinese Premier and Education Minister visit

Chinese Education Minister Huai Jinpeng visited campus on Friday as part of
Chinese Premier Li Qiang's visit to New Zealand. Minister Huai met with students
at the Confucius Institute and toured our labs at the Maurice Wilkins Centre for
Molecular Biodiscovery. I briefed the Minister on our partnership in China and
discussed the future of the New Zealand-China education relationship. Later, the
University signed cooperation agreements to support Chinese language learning at
the China-New Zealand Education Development Forum. You can read more about this
in the University hosts Chinese delegation article
