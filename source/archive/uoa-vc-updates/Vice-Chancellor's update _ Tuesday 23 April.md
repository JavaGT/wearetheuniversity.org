Kia ora koutou,

While we pause to remember and pay tribute on Anzac Day later this week, much of
our focus looks to the future.

Proposed new faculty arrangements

Thank you to everyone who has provided feedback on this proposal so far. If you
still need to do so, or wish to provide further input, please note that
the online feedback form
[[https://url.au.m.mimecastprotect.com/s/BgD4C0YKnXhWAAkxIwFLFa?domain=uoa.custhelp.com]](https://url.au.m.mimecastprotect.com/s/BgD4C0YKnXhWAAkxIwFLFa?domain=uoa.custhelp.com])
closes at 5pm today. Following this, submissions will be reviewed by a Review
Committee comprising both staff and students. Council will consider their
recommendations and advice from Senate in June.

Universitas 21 2024

The University will be represented at the Universitas 21 (U21) Annual Network
Meeting and Symposium this week. The theme for the meeting is 'Global
Universities and the Workforce of the Future.' We are one of four universities
selected to present case studies on this subject with out Centre for Innovation
and Entrepeneurship to be showcased.

Flu vaccines

A reminder that, with winter approaching, flu vaccines and Covid-19 boosters are
free to students, as well as permanent and fixed-term staff. Details on where
these are available can be found on the 2024 influence and vaccination for staff
and students notice page
[[https://url.au.m.mimecastprotect.com/s/oCf0CgZ0qQfWzzq3Io3yd0?domain=uoa.custhelp.com].](https://url.au.m.mimecastprotect.com/s/oCf0CgZ0qQfWzzq3Io3yd0?domain=uoa.custhelp.com].)
