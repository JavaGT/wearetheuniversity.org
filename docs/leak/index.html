Leak Information

If you have information you believe should be shared with WEARETHEUNIVERSITY, you can leak it safely and anonymously. Here’s how:

How to Leak Data Safely

  1. Remove Metadata:

    • Before sharing files, remove any metadata (author, location, device info) from documents, images, and videos.
    • Use tools like MAT2 or ExifTool to scrub metadata.

  2. Use Secure Channels:

    • Prefer encrypted email, secure drop services, or anonymous file sharing platforms.
    • Consider using ProtonMail or Tutanota for email.

  3. Anonymize Your Identity:

    • Use Tor Browser or a trusted VPN to hide your IP address.
    • Avoid sharing personal details or patterns that could identify you.

  4. Contact Us Securely:

    • Email: leak@wearetheuniversity.org
    • Or use our secure contact form (if available).

  5. Legal Notice:

    • Ensure you are not violating any laws or putting yourself at risk. Only share information you have the right to disclose.

If you have questions about safe leaking, contact us securely for guidance.
